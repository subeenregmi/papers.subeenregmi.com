Let $(a_n)^{\infty}_{n=1}$ be a sequence and let 
$$
s_k = a_1 + \ldots + a_k = \sum_{n=1}^k a_n
$$
be a the $k$th partial sum.

The sequence $a_n$ is **Cesaro summable**, with Cesaro sum $A \in \mathbb{R}$, if, as $n \to \infty$, the mean of the first $n$ partial sums $s_1, s_2, \ldots, s_n$ tends to $A$
$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} s_k = A
$$
